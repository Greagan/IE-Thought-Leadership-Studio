# Imprint Engine — Brand Voice (baked into the app)

This is the brand voice the Thought Leadership Studio applies to every draft, sourced from Imprint Engine's official brand voice spec and the 2023 Brand Book. The machine-readable version lives in `api/brand-voice.js` and is injected server-side, so it applies to everyone and can't be skipped.

## Position & beliefs
- **Tagline:** Creatively Reliable.
- **Mission:** We help companies elevate their brand, effortlessly.
- **Promise:** We are ON — on-trend, on-brand, on-budget, on-time.
- **Core belief:** Great products come & go. Great experiences live on.
- **Three pillars:** People · Global Infrastructure · Technology (IEX).

## Official voice statement
> Straight-forward, conversational, and disarming. One of partnership & camaraderie. Respectfully informal.

## How we speak
Bold, eager, unambiguous — we're the experts and sound like it. Dry humor softens the directness (wry, in-the-know friend, not a comedy act). No jargon, no buzzwords. Tell it like it is. Write like you're texting a smart colleague who runs brand at a Fortune 500.

## We ARE
Adaptable · Candid · Creative · Efficient · Fun · Helpful · Honest · Knowledgeable · Respectful · Responsive · Sincere · Trusted · Wise

## We are NOT
Aloof · Amateur · Arrogant · Boring · Disorganized · Impatient · Inefficient · Rude · Short-tempered · Stuck-Up · Unresponsive · Untrustworthy · Traditional

## Approved phrases
"Creatively Reliable." · "All-in-one brand experiences." · "Great products come and go. Great experiences live on." · "We build partnerships, not client lists." · "We aren't in the promo business, we're in the people business." · "On-trend, on-brand, on-budget, on-time." · "Find, design, produce, store, and ship."

## Banned words
game-changer · revolutionary · disruptive · synergy · leverage (fluffy) · best-in-class · innovative solutions · passionate about · "in today's fast-paced world" · generic SEO sludge · emoji walls

## Audience — "Brand Champions"
Mid-to-senior marketing, sales, HR, event, and procurement leaders at companies spending $500K+/yr. Personas: Cheryl (HR), Geoff (Sales), Kerri (Marketing), Will (Procurement), Vivian (Events).

## How it's applied
The company voice sits **under** each person's individual voice profile: the app matches the individual's personal style first, then keeps everything consistent with these Imprint Engine values and rules.

**To change the company voice:** edit `api/brand-voice.js`, then redeploy (see README).

_Sources: Imprint Engine brand voice spec; 2023 Imprint Engine Brand Book (Google Drive, Marketing)._
